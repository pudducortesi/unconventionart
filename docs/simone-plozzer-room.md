# Simone Plozzer — artist room

## Curatorial direction
Single-level room, approximately three times the width of a current exhibition room, planned for 75 works. The room translates Simone's supplied drawings into architecture: paper-white fields, irregular ink-black strokes, graphite and very restrained cobalt/cyan light accents.

## Artwork integrity
The three supplied works are visual references and first seed works. Keep the uploaded originals byte-for-byte; do not recolor, crop, redraw, denoise, upscale or generatively alter them. Derivatives may only be non-destructive display previews.

## Spatial grammar
Five freestanding divider sequences organize the room without creating separate rooms. Their language is informed by ALIVAR Museum / modular divider furniture, but geometry is original to UnconventionArt. Dividers should feel like gestural ink strokes in space rather than conventional partition walls.

## Publishing
Target role: artist. Simone can create, edit, publish and withdraw only works owned by Simone and assigned to room `simone-plozzer`. Capacity is 75. Museum architecture, other artists, user management and code remain owner-only.

Do not send an invitation until database ownership columns and Supabase RLS enforce this scope server-side.
