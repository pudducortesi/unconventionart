About / 01

A studio about proximity,
not distance.

Unconventional Studio is an independent photographic project built around a simple idea: beauty is not necessarily far, expensive or rare. Often it is one street away, half-lit, waiting to be looked at properly.

The premise

The project began as a private habit — two people walking, photographing, paying attention. A side street, a villa, a borgo, a lake at the wrong hour, a face caught in the right light. None of it produced. None of it staged.

What turned habit into project is the second step: a slow, editorial post-production that does not fabricate a different reality, but pushes the original frame toward its strongest version.

The method

Every image starts from a real photograph. From there, we work on light, contrast, color temperature and atmosphere — the same tools any director of photography uses, applied with patience to images that were not, at first, planned as cinema.

AI enters as an invisible studio: a quiet assistant that helps clean composition, refine mood and keep a consistent visual language across the archive. It never replaces the shot. It develops it.

Personal but presentable. Spontaneous but curated. Real, but amplified.

The format

The site is not a portfolio. It is an evolving archive — issues, stories, before-and-afters, places we keep returning to. New entries appear when there is something to say, not on a schedule.

Some images live alone. Some belong to a sequence. The archive reads like a magazine that never quite closes its current issue.

The method.

Five steps, repeated quietly. Nothing here is a secret — only the order, the patience and the refusal to do too much.

01

Walk / Explore

Step one — Movement

Short outings, nearby places, no production. We move on foot when we can. The unit of work is a walk, not a shoot. Most of our best frames belong to days we did not plan as photographic days.

02

Shoot

Step two — Attention

Spontaneous capture with quiet attention to faces, light, architecture and atmosphere. We choose the camera that disappears in the hand. We do not interrupt the moment to fix it.

03

Select

Step three — Editing the eye

Most images are discarded. We keep the frames with narrative weight — the ones that suggest a sequence even when alone. AI helps speed the cull, but the final selection is always human and slow.

04

Direct

Step four — Mood

For each story we commit to a register: editorial, cinematic, fashion, minimal, noir, romantic, urban. The choice is made before color work, not during it. Direction is decision, not effect.

05

Post-produce

Step five — Translation

Light, color, cleanup, coherence. Post-production translates the chosen mood into pixels. It does not invent a different reality — it gives the original frame a stronger visual identity, image after image, until the archive feels like one voice.
